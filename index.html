Screen settings

On a phone, tap Settings, Athkaar, or Announcements at the top. On a keyboard, press S for settings, A for athkaar, and Escape to close. Iqamah, the masjid name, added athkaar, and the reminders are saved on this computer only until this screen is opened from the website. Reminders of what not to do cover the screen for a few seconds each minute, and they pause during iqamah and salah.
